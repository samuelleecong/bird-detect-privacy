Privacy Policy

Bird Detect — AI-powered bird photo cropping for macOS

Last Updated: May 4, 2026
TL;DR: Bird Detect runs 100% on your Mac. Your photos never leave your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Purchases are processed by Apple, not by us.

Our Privacy Commitment

Bird Detect is designed to be private by default. There is no account, no cloud, and no tracking. All bird detection runs locally on your Mac using Apple's CoreML framework with a bundled machine-learning model.

What Bird Detect Accesses on Your Device

The app requests the following macOS permissions, only to perform its core function:

  • Photos Library: If you select a Photos album, the app reads images from your library to detect birds. Crops are written back to Photos as reversible adjustments — your originals are never deleted, and you can revert any photo at any time. The app never uploads your photos.
  • File Access (user-selected folders): If you select a folder of images instead of a Photos album, the app reads from that folder and writes cropped output to a folder you choose. Access is granted only to the folders you explicitly pick via the macOS file picker.

That's it. No camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no network access for detection.

Photo Processing

All bird detection and image cropping occur 100% locally on your Mac using Apple's CoreML framework. The machine-learning model is bundled with the app. There are no API calls, no cloud services, and no data transmission of any kind during detection or cropping.

Cropped images are saved either to a folder you pick or, when working from a Photos album, applied to your Photos library as a non-destructive adjustment that can be reverted at any time using Apple's standard Photos editing tools.

In-App Purchases

Bird Detect offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock the full version. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple's App Store and StoreKit framework. Bird Detect does not see, store, or transmit any payment information.

Apple may collect purchase-related information as described in Apple's Privacy Policy. The app verifies your purchase status locally using StoreKit's signed transactions; no purchase data is sent to Bird Detect's developer.

Promotional and Offer Codes are issued and redeemed entirely through Apple — Bird Detect itself does not implement or store any redemption logic.

Analytics & Tracking

We use no analytics, tracking, telemetry, or advertising tools. There are:

  • No Google Analytics, Firebase, or similar SDKs
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA) collected
  • No crash reporting (unless you explicitly send a report via email)
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind

Children's Privacy

Bird Detect is rated 4+ and does not collect any personal information from any user, regardless of age. There are no special considerations needed for users under 13 because no data is ever collected.

Data Retention & Security

Because we do not collect data, there is nothing to retain or secure on our end. Your photos, projects, and cropped output remain under your control on your Mac.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the "Last Updated" date above will reflect the change. We will not begin collecting any data without an updated policy and clear in-app disclosure.
